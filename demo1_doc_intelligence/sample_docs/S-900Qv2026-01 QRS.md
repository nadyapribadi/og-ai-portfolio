# Quality Requirements for Deluge Skid Assemblies

This is a synthetic document written for this demo. It is not a real
specification and must not be used for procurement.

<!-- page -->
## 1 Scope

This document states the quality requirements that apply to the supply of
deluge skid assemblies. It is read together with the technical requirements
specification S-900.

## 2 Quality requirements

2.1.1 A test certificate shall be provided for each pressure-containing
component.

2.1.2 Material certificates shall be traceable to the heat number.

2.1.3 Welding procedures shall be qualified before the start of fabrication.

<!-- page -->
## 3 Inspection and testing

### 3.1 Inspection

3.1.1 The purchaser shall be given access to the works for inspection at any
reasonable time during fabrication.

3.1.2 Inspection points shall be recorded in the inspection and test plan.

### 3.2 Pressure testing

3.2.1 Each skid shall be hydrostatically tested at 1.5 times the design
pressure for a minimum of 30 minutes.

3.2.2 The test medium shall be water unless the procurement data sheet
specifies otherwise.

<!-- page -->
## 4 Documentation

4.1.1 The manufacturer shall provide a manufacturing record book containing
material certificates, welding procedure specifications and qualification
records, pressure test certificates, coating inspection reports, and the final
inspection and test plan with results recorded.

4.1.2 Documentation shall be supplied in English unless otherwise agreed.

## 5 Conformity assessment

5.1.1 Conformity assessment activities shall be completed before shipment.

5.1.2 Any deviation from S-900 shall be raised as a concession request and
resolved before shipment.
