# Supplementary Specification to ISO 9900 — Deluge Skid Assemblies

This is a synthetic document written for this demo. It is not a real
specification and must not be used for procurement. It exists so that the
project can be run from a fresh clone without redistributing a copyrighted
industry standard.

<!-- page -->
## 1 Scope

This specification covers the design, manufacture, inspection and testing
requirements of deluge skid assemblies for onshore and offshore oil and gas
applications. It is limited to deluge type packages without foam.

The following are outside the scope of this specification:

- firewater pumps;
- nozzles and other discharge devices downstream of the deluge valve;
- fire and gas detection.

## 3 Definitions

For the purposes of this document the following definitions apply.

3.1.1 deluge valve
interface through which the firewater supply is released into the skid piping.

3.1.2 winterization
measures implemented to reduce the effects of low ambient temperature on the
equipment.

## 4 Design requirements

### 4.1 Enclosure and ingress protection

4.1.1 Electrical equipment enclosures shall be at least IP56 in accordance with
IEC 60529.

4.1.2 Instrument junction boxes and electrical junction boxes shall have tag
plates installed on the outside of the enclosure.

### 4.2 Pressure design

4.2.1 The deluge skid shall be designed for a minimum design pressure of 16 bar
unless a higher pressure is stated in the procurement data sheet.

4.2.2 Pressure-containing components shall be rated for the design pressure of
the skid at the maximum design temperature.

### 4.3 Electrical installations

4.3.1 Cables within the deluge skid shall be terminated in a junction box and
shall be supported at intervals not exceeding 300 mm.

4.3.2 Cables shall be tested in accordance with IEC 60332.

## 5 Protective coating, painting and insulation

### 5.1 Protective coatings

5.1.1 Surface preparation for steel structures shall be in accordance with
ISO 12944-4.

5.1.2 Execution and supervision of the coating application shall be in
accordance with ISO 12944-7 and the coating system procedure specification.

### 5.2 Insulation

5.2.1 Where insulation is provided it shall be removable.

## 6 Marking

6.1 Each skid shall carry a stainless steel tag plate showing the tag number,
the design pressure and the year of manufacture.
